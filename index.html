About VICECITY HUB

An independent fan-made Grand Theft Auto community project.

VICECITY HUB is an unofficial fan website built for Grand Theft Auto players and the wider GTA community.

The goal is to create one searchable destination for characters, vehicles, weapons, missions, locations, guides, screenshots, videos and GTA history.

The database is designed to grow continuously as new official information becomes available.
